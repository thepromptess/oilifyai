ABOUT OILIFY AI

🇬🇾 Proudly Guyanese — Built in New York

Oilify AI was founded by a Guyanese entrepreneur who brought the resilience and resourcefulness of the Caribbean coast to the heart of New York City's industrial economy.

Rooted in Guyana's rich oil-and-gas heritage — a nation transformed by discovery — we channel that same energy into building AI-driven tools that modernize logistics and workforce training for heavy industry across the Americas.

From the Demerara River to the Hudson, we bring a relentless work ethic, deep industry knowledge, and cutting-edge artificial intelligence to every client engagement.
